## What is division management?

The BARS division management system gives your division control over the airport lighting data for airports within your jurisdiction. Your nominated staff define the lighting layout, maintain its properties, and decide when an airport is ready for scenery contributions.

One shared layout keeps airport data accurate and consistent. Controllers and scenery contributors can refer to the same lighting objects instead of maintaining conflicting definitions for each simulator or scenery package.

## What your division manages

- Staff access through the Nav Head and Nav Member roles.
- Requests to manage airports within your jurisdiction.
- Airport lighting locations, names, types, and lighting properties.
- Whether each approved airport accepts scenery contributions.

BARS staff set up divisions and review airport access requests. Scenery contributors use your division's data to position lights in individual simulator scenery packages.

## Start with your division

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your division">
    [Contact BARS](/divisions/getting-started) from your division email address and nominate the first Nav Head.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add staff and request airports">
    [Manage access](/divisions/management) and wait for airport approval before adding lighting data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the airport layout">
    Define the [lighting objects and their properties](/divisions/airport-data). Use the editor appropriate to your controller client.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open scenery contributions">
    Once the complete airport layout has been checked and uploaded, enable contributions so contributors can add scenery support.
  </Step>
</Steps>
