## Request division access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Email BARS from your division address">
    Send an email to [contact@stopbars.com](mailto:contact@stopbars.com) from your division email address, such as an address ending in `@vatpac.org`.

    Ask for your division to be added to BARS. Include your division's name, your staff position, and your VATSIM CID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for setup">
    The BARS team will set up your division and add the person who first contacted us as its initial Nav Head. That person can then add other staff as Nav Heads or Nav Members.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open division management">
    Sign in to the [BARS website](https://stopbars.com) with the VATSIM account for the CID you supplied. Open your account page and select **Manage Division** for your division.

    If your division is missing, check that you used the correct CID and contact BARS for help.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add staff and request airports">
    Follow [Staff and airports](/divisions/management) to add your team and request the airports you will maintain. Airport access must be approved before you can add lighting data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Join the division support channels

We recommend that your division staff join the [BARS Discord server](https://stopbars.com/discord).

1. Run the BARS Discord Bot's `/division` command in any channel to request the division role.
2. Enter the requested details and proof of your position.
3. Wait for the BARS team to review your request and grant access.

The role gives you access to private channels for division members. You can speak directly with BARS staff, get support, and receive division news and announcements.

<Note>
  Discord channel access and website division membership are separate. Your Nav Head manages website access through the division management page.
</Note>

## Prepare your first airport

Gather current airport charts and reliable information about the lighting layout. Agree who will maintain the data and check changes before opening scenery contributions.

Divisions using vatSys can follow the [website editor guide](/divisions/website-editor). Divisions using EuroScope should follow the [EuroScope guide](/divisions/euroscope-guide).
