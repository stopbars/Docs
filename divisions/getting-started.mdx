## Request division access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Email BARS from your division address">
    Send an email to [contact@stopbars.com](mailto:contact@stopbars.com) from your division email address, eg: `@vatpac.org`.

    Ask for your division to be added to BARS. Include your division's name, your staff position, and your VATSIM CID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for setup">
    The BARS team will set up your division and add the person who first contacted us as its initial Nav Head. That person can then add other staff as Nav Heads or Nav Members.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open division management">
    We'll reply when your division is set up. Log in to the [BARS website](https://stopbars.com) with your VATSIM account, open your account page, and scroll down to **Your Divisions**, then Click **Manage Division**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/v2/Divisions-Your-Divisions.png" alt="Your Divisions card showing your division, staff role, and Manage Division button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add staff and request airports">
    Follow [Staff and airports](/divisions/management), airport access must be approved before you can add lighting data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Join the division support channels

We recommend that your division staff join the [BARS Discord server](https://stopbars.com/discord).

1. Run the BARS Discord Bot's `/division role` command in any channel.
2. Enter the requested details and proof of your position.
3. Wait for the BARS team to review your request and grant access.

The role gives you access to private channels for division members. You can speak directly with BARS staff, get support, and receive division news and announcements.
