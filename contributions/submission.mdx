Once you've checked the browser preview and tested the lighting in your simulator, submit your contribution for review. Add the final scenery details, then track its status from the Contribution Dashboard.

## Complete the Final Details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name the scenery package">
    Enter the package or developer name contributors will recognize, such as `FlyTampa`, `iniBuilds`, or `Default`. Matching package suggestions appear while you type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the simulator">
    Choose **MSFS 2024**, **MSFS 2020**, or **X-Plane**. It must match the simulator used to create and test the draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add review notes">
    Notes are optional. Include any details the reviewer needs, such as a known scenery limitation or the reason for replacing an older contribution.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the tested draft">
    The status card must say **Tested draft ready** and name the expected file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Ready to submit">
    Confirm that you followed the guide, tested the draft, and created the work yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Submit contribution">
    BARS uploads the tested draft and adds it to the review queue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>

## Review Process

After submission, the BARS team will review your contribution. It remains pending until reviewed, with two possible outcomes:

| Outcome | What Happens |
| --- | --- |
| Approved | Your contribution is accepted and immediately available for use by pilots. |
| Rejected | Your contribution is not accepted. Open **View Reason** to read the reviewer's feedback. |

A rejection reason explains the decision when the reviewer supplied one. If your contribution is rejected and you are unsure why, or wish to dispute the decision, join the [BARS Discord server](https://stopbars.com/discord) and contact staff with your contribution ID.

## Tracking Your Contributions

You can track the status of all your contributions through the [Contribution Dashboard](https://stopbars.com/contribute) under the **Your Contributions** tab.

From this tab, you can:

- See total, approved, pending, and rejected counts.
- Search by airport, scenery, or simulator.
- Read the last-updated time and simulator for each submission.
- Delete a pending or rejected contribution.
- Open the reason attached to a rejected contribution.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/v2/Contributions-Manage.png" alt="Your Contributions dashboard with approved, pending, and rejected status totals plus View Reason and Delete actions" />
</Frame>

<Note>
  If you need to remove an approved contribution from BARS, contact the team at [support@stopbars.com](mailto:support@stopbars.com) with your contribution ID and reason for removal.
</Note>

## Display Name

Your name is publicly visible on the Contribution Dashboard to all visitors. You can control how your name appears publicly across BARS.

Open your [account settings](https://stopbars.com/account) and under **Preferred Display Name** mode, select one of:

- First Name
- First Name + Last Initial
- VATSIM CID

The Contribution Dashboard will use your chosen display name.

## Top Contributors

The Top Contributors leaderboard on the Contribution Dashboard recognises users who submit the most contributions. It’s our way of saying thank you and giving recognition to the community members who go above and beyond in expanding airport support.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I do not understand a rejection">
    Open **View Reason** first. If the reason is still unclear, ask in the [BARS Discord server](https://stopbars.com/discord) and include the contribution ID. Do not post private account details.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I submitted the wrong scenery or simulator">
    Delete the pending submission from **Your Contributions**, return to the contribution flow, and submit a tested draft with the correct details.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I need to replace an approved contribution">
    Create and test a new draft for the same airport, scenery, and simulator. Explain the replacement in the review notes.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
