The submission page accepts the draft prepared by the test step. Complete the final details, confirm the acknowledgement, and send it to the BARS review team.

## Complete the final details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name the scenery package">
    Enter the package or developer name contributors will recognize, such as `FlyTampa`, `iniBuilds`, or `Default`. Matching package suggestions appear while you type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the simulator">
    Choose **MSFS 2024**, **MSFS 2020**, or **X-Plane**. It must match the simulator used to create and test the draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add review notes">
    Notes are optional. Include anything the reviewer cannot infer from the draft, such as a known scenery limitation or the reason for replacing an older contribution.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the tested draft">
    The status card must say **Tested draft ready** and name the expected file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Ready to submit">
    Confirm that you followed the guide, tested the draft, and created the work yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Submit contribution">
    BARS uploads the tested draft and adds it to the review queue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review outcomes

| Status | What it means | Available actions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pending | The contribution is waiting for a staff decision. | View it in **Your Contributions** or delete it. |
| Approved | The contribution passed review and is available to pilots. | Download its XML from **All Contributions**. |
| Rejected | The reviewer found an issue that needs another submission. | View the rejection reason or delete the record. |

A rejection reason explains the decision when the reviewer supplied one. Correct the draft and submit a new contribution rather than trying to edit the rejected record.

## Manage your submissions

Open the [Contribution Dashboard](https://preview.stopbars.com/contribute), then select **Your Contributions**. You can:

- See total, approved, pending, and rejected counts.
- Search by airport, scenery, or simulator.
- Read the last-updated time and simulator for each submission.
- Delete a pending or rejected contribution.
- Open the reason attached to a rejected contribution.

Deleting a pending or rejected contribution permanently removes its contribution and scenery data. The confirmation dialog names the airport and scenery before deletion.

<Note>
  The dashboard cannot delete an approved contribution. Contact `support@stopbars.com` with the contribution ID and the reason it should be removed.
</Note>

## Public contributor name

The dashboard shows the contributor name attached to approved scenery. In [account settings](https://preview.stopbars.com/account), choose the preferred display name mode:

- First name.
- First name and last initial.
- VATSIM CID.

The dashboard also includes a Top Contributors leaderboard. Connect Discord to your BARS account if you want the contributor role in the BARS Discord server.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I do not understand a rejection">
    Open **View Reason** first. If the reason is still unclear, ask in the [BARS Discord server](https://stopbars.com/discord) and include the contribution ID. Do not post private account details.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I submitted the wrong scenery or simulator">
    Delete the pending submission from **Your Contributions**, return to the contribution flow, and submit a tested draft with the correct details.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I need to replace an approved contribution">
    Create and test a new draft for the same airport, scenery, and simulator. Explain the replacement in the review notes.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
